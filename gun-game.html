Signal on the torus

Your rig tags you as debt-runner 9-K — not a name, a balance. The arena does not care about fair. It cares about throughput. Every clear wave is a receipt stamped against a ledger you have never seen.

The targets are not “aliens.” They are cheap pursuers: same code, new bodies, old grudge. The torus loops so you cannot run forever, only manage the panic. When something brushes close, the floor rewards a juke with a surge — two seconds where the hunters sprint as if they remembered a reason to hate you.

Survive. Spend what you earn in the shop like a citizen. And if the static on your band starts spelling words, pretend you did not notice — until the next wave arrives.
